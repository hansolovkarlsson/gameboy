# 2026-09-04

The emulator did not change today. The way the project keeps its own account of
itself did, and then that new account was used for the first time — to find out
where the work actually stands after two and a half weeks away from it.

## Splitting the roadmap without rewriting it

[GAMEBOY_ROADMAP.md](../GAMEBOY_ROADMAP.md) had been doing four jobs at once
across 5,353 lines: the plan, the record of what exists, the scoring of what
went wrong, and the note of when a thing became visible from outside. That is
not a criticism of how it was written — its Status section is genuinely good,
grounded prose, and the Phase 8 entry that says a real timing fix *did not*
close `dmg-acid2`'s gap is the kind of honesty a document has to earn. The
problem was only that finding any one of those four things meant reading past
the other three.

So [POSTMORTEM.md](../POSTMORTEM.md) and [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md) were
split out, and [work-journal/](.) created as a directory rather than a page.

The decision worth recording is what the split deliberately did *not* do: it
did not move the existing entries. Every postmortem-shaped paragraph written
before today stays in the roadmap's Status section, and POSTMORTEM.md's own
opening note says so and points at Phase 8 as the example of what it would have
collected. The alternative — migrating years of entries into the new file — was
rejected because it would have broken every internal cross-reference in a
document that leans heavily on them ("see the Next section above", "the same
technique the LCD-enable-quirk investigation used"), and because re-voicing an
entry its author already got right is how a record acquires errors it did not
have. A split that starts from today and admits it starts from today is worth
more than a tidy-looking one that quietly re-writes what came before.

The three new records are `nav_exclude`d in [_config.yml](../_config.yml) rather
than moved out of `docs/`. `docs/` is the published GitHub Pages site, so
`nav_exclude` only takes a page out of the sidebar — it is still built and still
reachable by URL. That is the intended outcome: these are written for whoever
works on the emulator, not for the guide's readers, but there is no reason to
hide them from someone who goes looking.

`make test` gained a definition as an alias for `gameboy-test`. It is
deliberately not a superset — the ROM-based regressions (`gameboy-visual-test`
and the byte-exact game tests) stay separate because they are slower and because
they are exactly the targets worth naming individually when one of them is what
you are chasing. The reason the alias exists at all is that tooling arriving at
this repo cold looks for `make test` first, and without it a passing suite reads
as an absent one.

## What the audit found

With the records in place, the obvious next question was what they say. The
answer is that this project has run out of queued work, which was not obvious
before asking.

All twelve phases are done. The unit suite is green — 0 failures across CPU,
timer, APU and save state, run today. Every `Next` note in the roadmap's Status
section has been consumed: Ascent's Milestone 8 entry closes by observing that
score, sound and lives were the entire list Milestone 4 first raised and all
three have landed, so anything further "would be a genuinely new scope". The
same is true of Wayfarer. Nothing is waiting.

What remains open is three named, deliberately-scoped gaps rather than a
backlog: the Mooneye timer cluster (80/83, the three failures being
`rapid_toggle.gb`, `tima_write_reloading.gb`, `tma_write_reloading.gb`);
Blargg's `dmg_sound` at 8/12, where `08-len ctr during power` was narrowed by
real M-cycle tracing but not isolated, and `09`/`10`/`12` all exercise Wave RAM
mid-playback behaviour that `apu.h` has declared out of scope since Phase 5; and
`dmg-acid2`'s small residual visual gap. Each of those is documented in the
Status section with the evidence for why it is where it is, which is why none of
them needed to be re-derived today.

The audit also turned up `test_roms/tobutobugirl/tobu.gb.sav` sitting untracked
since 17 August. It is battery RAM written by `make gameboy-tobu-test` when it
runs the ROM — generated output, not a reference, unlike the `reference_*.sav`
files in `prism/` and `wayfarer/` that are committed on purpose. It wants a
`.gitignore` line rather than a commit.
