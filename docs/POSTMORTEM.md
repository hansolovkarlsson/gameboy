# Postmortem

*The **scoring**. One entry per mistake or prediction that has met evidence —
one that held, one that failed, or one that held and then failed — under
**Issue**, **Root cause**, **Solution**, **Learnings**. Not a bug log: a defect
belongs here when what it taught outlives it, and a day that scored nothing
adds nothing. The learning is the part that has to be true a year from now, so
it says what would have caught the thing rather than resolving to be more
careful.*

Nothing has been scored on this page yet — it was split out from
[GAMEBOY_ROADMAP.md](GAMEBOY_ROADMAP.md), which had been doing this job in
prose and still holds every entry from before today. Its `Status` section is
the place to look for the earlier scoring: Phase 8's real, additive timing fix
that turned out **not** to close `dmg-acid2`'s remaining visual gap is the
clearest example of what this page collects, and it stays where it is rather
than being copied here. New scoring goes here; the roadmap keeps what is
already written in it.
